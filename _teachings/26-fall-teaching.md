## Course Content

Comparing to undergraduate probability theory taught by other brother universities, our university's probability theory for math major includes more contents and considers problems under real analysis(advanced probability theory). Our course mainly contains:

- Probability space and independence;
- Discrete random variable, continuous random variable, expectation and moments of random variables, conditional distribution and expectation, Normal Distribution's applications;
- Probability methods, random walk;
- Simple Abstract integral, convergence of random variables;
- Generating and characteristic functions, Komolgorov Strong Law of Large Numbers, Central Limit Theorem, method of moments.<br>

Probability theory is a branch of mathematics concerned with primary concepts，theories and methods of random phenomena and provides a necessary basis for statistics. This is one of the most fundemental courses in this branch.
