## Calendar rules

- Week 1 starts on Monday, August 31, 2026, in Asia/Shanghai (UTC+8).
- Monday classes meet 14:00–15:35 in weeks 1–5 and 7–15.
- Thursday classes meet 09:45–11:20 in weeks 1–4 and 6–15.
- October 1 and October 5 are explicitly shown as no-class dates.
- The first lecture covers Chapter 0 as a whole. The next 24 lectures follow
  sections 1.1–1.6, 2.1–2.6, 3.1–3.4, 4.1–4.4, and 5.1–5.4, in order.
- Chapter problem sections and Chapters 6 onward are excluded from lectures.
- Section 5.4 falls on November 30. December 3, 7, and 10 are in-class recitations.
- Eight Saturday recitations meet 15:55–18:20: September 12 and 26; October 17
  and 31; November 14 and 28; December 12 and 26. The last four dates continue
  the user's two-week pattern after October 31. The final session is in week 17.
- Exam dates, homework deadlines, and later recitation topics are unspecified.
  Do not invent them or reserve a lecture for an exam without an update.

The grade is 20% homework (including attendance), 30% midterm, and 50% final.
