# MATH3007.01 · Fall 2026 schedule

The course page is `_teachings/26-fall-teaching.md`. Its dated sessions are in
`_data/math3007_2026.yml`, ordered chronologically. Edit that data file to change
topics, dates, reading references, or material links.

Weekly homework groups and answer links are in `_data/math3007_homework_2026.yml`.
The page derives the exercise sets from each group's lecture sections, so moving
a lecture automatically moves its homework to the corresponding week.

## Calendar rules

- Week 1 starts on Monday, August 31, 2026, in Asia/Shanghai (UTC+8).
- Monday classes meet 14:00–15:35 in weeks 1–5 and 7–15.
- Thursday classes meet 09:45–11:20 in weeks 1–4 and 6–15.
- October 1 and October 5 are explicitly shown as no-class dates.
- The first lecture covers Chapter 0 as a whole. The next 24 lectures follow
  sections 1.1–1.6, 2.1–2.6, 3.1–3.4, 4.1–4.4, and 5.1–5.4, in order.
- Chapter problem sections and Chapters 6 onward are excluded from lectures.
- Section 5.4 falls on November 30. December 3, 7, and 10 are in-class recitations.
- Eight Saturday recitations meet 15:55–18:20: September 12 and 26; October 17
  and 31; November 14 and 28; December 12 and 26. The last four dates continue
  the user's two-week pattern after October 31. The final session is in week 17.
- Exam dates, exact homework deadlines, and later recitation topics are unspecified.
  Do not invent them or reserve a lecture for an exam without an update.

The grade is 20% homework (including attendance), 30% midterm, and 50% final.

## Weekly homework

- Submit once per teaching week, combining weeks 1–2 into Homework 1.
- Homework 2–14 correspond to weeks 3–15. Week 17 is a standalone recitation.
- Assign the end-of-section exercise sets headed `习题` in the textbook for
  every section taught during that group. Chapter 0 has no exercise set.
- Homework 1 therefore covers `习题 1.1`, `习题 1.2`, and `习题 1.3`.
- Week 15 has only review sessions; its review exercises remain to be announced.
- Each group has one homework cell and one answer cell spanning its sessions.
  On small screens, these appear beneath the first session of the group.
- Row placement indicates coverage, not a submission deadline. No due weekday
  or time has been supplied.
- Populate a group's `answers` field with a published solution URL when ready,
  for example `/files/teaching/math3007-2026/homework-01-answers.pdf`. Until then,
  the page shows “Not posted” without a broken or placeholder link.

## Source materials

Section names and printed page ranges were checked against the table of contents
on PDF pages 3–4 of `PTbook20260831.pdf`. Its cover is dated September 2, 2026,
and marks the book for USTC internal use. The full textbook and extracted pages
are not included in either repository or the public website.

The original `概率论习题课讲义 0912.pdf` is served unchanged as
`files/teaching/math3007-2026/recitation-2026-09-12.pdf`. Its cover date is
September 9 (preparation date); the course session remains September 12, as
specified by the user. Its contents page identifies the first session's
mathematical analysis and measure-theory preliminaries.

The schedule layout is based on the dated topic/material table at
[Stanford CS224W](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224w/index.html#schedule),
with the site's existing typography and mobile layout.
The separate navigation controls were also informed by
[Stanford CS231n](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/), and the pairing of problem sets
and solutions by [Harvard Stat 110](https://stat110.hsites.harvard.edu/strategic-practice-problems).
