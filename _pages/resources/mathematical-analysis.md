This part will include notes taken in the Mathematical Analysis in the first three semesters. Mathematical Analysis I&II are courses taken by Prof. HuTing Wang who was retired in Spring, 2025. For Mathematical Analysis III, this is a course for students who shifted their major into Mathematics taught by Prof. Bin Xu(including students from the school of the gifted young). You will experience the most brilliant part in Mathematical Analysis, including but not limited to:

- Arzela-Ascoli Theorem, Weierstrass Theorem
- Basic Topology
- Implicit Function Theorem, Inverse Function Theorem, Rank Theorem
- Jordan measure, Jacobian change-of-variables formula

The notes cover all the content in the courses and some reviews for exams.<br>
